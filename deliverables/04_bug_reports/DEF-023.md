# DEF-023: 6% Service Tax Rounds Half-Sen (.xx5) Values Down Instead of Half-Up (`MEJ-TOT-04`)

## WBT Form Fields

- **Summary \*:** 6% Service tax rounds half-sen (.xx5) values down instead of half-up, undercharging tax by RM0.01 (MEJ-TOT-04)
- **Area of Impact \*:** Tray Totals & Tax Calculation (MEJ-TOT-04)
- **Issue Type \*:** Functional
- **Severity \*:** Critical
- **Priority \*:** High
- **Frequency \*:** Always
- **Product Risk \*:** High
- **Device Type:** Desktop
- **OS Version:** CachyOS Linux
- **Browser Type:** Chrome

---

### Description \*
Per `MEJ-TOT-04`, Service tax is `6% of (Items + Containers + Service charge), rounded half-up to the sen`. However, whenever the exact 6% service tax calculation ends in half a sen (`.xx5`), the server rounds **down** instead of half-up, consistently undercharging Service tax and the resulting tray total by `RM0.01`.

Verified across three independent `.xx5` boundary cases at Dewan Sejuk (Table 85, 10% Dine-in Service charge):
1. Items `RM2.50` + 10% Service charge `RM0.25` = Taxable base `RM2.75`. Exact 6% tax is `RM0.165`. Statutory half-up = `RM0.17`, but SUT computes `RM0.16` (Tray total `RM2.91` instead of `RM2.92`).
2. Items `RM7.50` + 10% Service charge `RM0.75` = Taxable base `RM8.25`. Exact 6% tax is `RM0.495`. Statutory half-up = `RM0.50`, but SUT computes `RM0.49` (Tray total `RM8.74` instead of `RM8.75`).
3. Items `RM12.50` + 10% Service charge `RM1.25` = Taxable base `RM13.75`. Exact 6% tax is `RM0.825`. Statutory half-up = `RM0.83`, but SUT computes `RM0.82` (Tray total `RM14.57` instead of `RM14.58`).

Because both odd (`0.165` -> `0.16`, `0.495` -> `0.49`) and even (`0.825` -> `0.82`) second decimal places round down, this confirms systematic round-down / truncation rather than statutory half-up rounding, affecting both Diner Balance and Cash at cashier totals.

---

### Steps to Reproduce \*
1. Sign in as a diner and clear or start a Draft tray.
2. Add 1 x `Kopi` (`RM2.50`) as `Dine in`, and select Table `85` (Dewan Sejuk, 10% Service charge hall).
3. Inspect the tray totals breakdown (`Service charge`, `Service tax`, `Tray total`).
4. Repeat with 3 x `Kopi` (`RM7.50`) and 5 x `Kopi` (`RM12.50`) on Table `85`.

---

### Expected Result \*
Per `MEJ-TOT-04`, 6% Service tax rounds half-up to the nearest sen:
- Base `RM2.75` (`0.165`): Service tax `RM0.17`, Tray total `RM2.92`.
- Base `RM8.25` (`0.495`): Service tax `RM0.50`, Tray total `RM8.75`.
- Base `RM13.75` (`0.825`): Service tax `RM0.83`, Tray total `RM14.58`.

---

### Actual Result \*
The SUT rounds `.xx5` Service tax values down by `RM0.01` across all three cases:
- Base `RM2.75`: Service tax `RM0.16`, Tray total `RM2.91`.
- Base `RM8.25`: Service tax `RM0.49`, Tray total `RM8.74`.
- Base `RM13.75`: Service tax `RM0.82`, Tray total `RM14.57`.

---
