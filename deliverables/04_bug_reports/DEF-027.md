# DEF-027: Send Tray Succeeds for Dishes from a Stall Marked "Closed for today"

- **Severity:** Critical
- **Module / Route:** `/sut/mejamarmar/tray/send` vs `/sut/mejamarmar/dapur/kitchen/4`
- **Spec Reference:** `MEJ-STL-04` (`Close for today`), `MEJ-CTR-06`
- **Verified By:** Live browser verification (`76f25aab`, steps 40-50)

## Steps to Reproduce
1. As Diner `Farid Iskandar`, hold `1 x Char kuey teow` (Stall 4: `Char Kuey Teow Ah Kheong`) in `My tray` with `Table 15` selected.
2. In Dapur, sign in as Manager `Rosnah Abdullah`, open Stall 4 (`Char Kuey Teow Ah Kheong`), and click `"Close for today"` and confirm.
3. Switch back to Diner `Farid Iskandar` on `/sut/mejamarmar/tray/send`, choose payment method, and click `"Send tray"`.

## Expected Result
Per `MEJ-STL-04`: `"A stall closed today says 'Closed today'; its dishes cannot be added ('<stall> is closed today.'), and a tray holding them cannot be sent: '<stall> is closed today. Please remove its dishes.'"`

## Actual Result
`Send tray` succeeds and creates `Tray T-1072` (`Awaiting payment`, `Pay code 6739`, `RM10.60`) for Stall 4 despite Stall 4 being marked `"Closed today"`.
