# DEF-014: Missing Single-Click "Tapau" and "Dine in" Buttons on Tray Lines (`MEJ-SEC-05`, `MEJ-TRY-03`)

## WBT Form Fields

- **Summary \*:** Missing single-click "Tapau" and "Dine in" buttons on tray lines (MEJ-SEC-05, MEJ-TRY-03)
- **Area of Impact \*:** Diner My Tray / Accessibility & Pointer Controls (MEJ-SEC-05)
- **Issue Type \*:** UI
- **Severity \*:** Major
- **Priority \*:** Medium
- **Frequency \*:** Always
- **Product Risk \*:** Medium
- **Device Type:** Desktop
- **OS Version:** CachyOS Linux
- **Browser Type:** Chrome

---

### Description \*
On the `My tray` page (`/sut/mejamarmar/tray`), dish lines can be switched between `Dine in` and `Tapau` only via drag-and-drop or keyboard `Space` pickup/drop. The single-click `"Tapau"` and `"Dine in"` buttons required by the specification are missing from the dish lines.

Per `MEJ-TRY-03` and `MEJ-SEC-05`, everything done by dragging on `/sut/mejamarmar/tray` must also be doable with a single click via the line's `"Tapau"` and `"Dine in"` buttons (consistent with WCAG 2.2 SC 2.5.7 Dragging Movements).

---

### Steps to Reproduce \*
1. Sign in to Meja Marmar (`/sut/mejamarmar/sign-in`) as a diner.
2. Add at least one dish to `My tray` from `/sut/mejamarmar/stalls`.
3. Navigate to `/sut/mejamarmar/tray`.
4. Inspect the controls rendered on each dish line for single-click `"Tapau"` and `"Dine in"` buttons.

---

### Expected Result \*
Per `MEJ-TRY-03` and `MEJ-SEC-05`, each dish line on `/sut/mejamarmar/tray` provides single-click `"Tapau"` and `"Dine in"` buttons so users who cannot perform dragging movements can move items between sections with a single pointer click.

---

### Actual Result \*
Only drag-and-drop and keyboard `Space` pickup/drop interactions exist on `/sut/mejamarmar/tray`. The single-click `"Tapau"` and `"Dine in"` buttons are absent from dish lines.

---

### Additional Information
- **Specification References:** `MEJ-TRY-03`, `MEJ-SEC-05` (Single-pointer alternative to dragging movements).
- **Affected URL:** `https://www.worldbesttester.com/sut/mejamarmar/tray`
