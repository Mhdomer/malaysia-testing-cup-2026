# DEF-019: Cashier "Take payment" Accepts Payment for Trays Holding a Busy Stall (MEJ-STL-05, MEJ-CTR-06, MEJ-PAY-04)

## WBT Form Fields

- **Summary \*:** Cashier "Take payment" accepts payment for trays holding a Busy stall (MEJ-STL-05, MEJ-CTR-06, MEJ-PAY-04)
- **Area of Impact \*:** Dapur / Payments & Stall Capacity (MEJ-STL-05, MEJ-CTR-06)
- **Issue Type \*:** Functional
- **Severity \*:** Critical
- **Priority \*:** High
- **Frequency \*:** Always
- **Product Risk \*:** High
- **Device Type:** Desktop
- **OS Version:** CachyOS Linux
- **Browser Type:** Chrome

---

### Description \*
Per `MEJ-STL-05`, `MEJ-CTR-06`, and `MEJ-PAY-04`, when a stall reaches 15 or more open tickets (`New`, `Cooking`, `Ready`), its status becomes `Busy` and it must stop accepting new orders. At `Take payment`, the cashier payment flow must re-check `Busy` status for every stall on the tray and refuse payment with `"<stall> is busy with <n> orders. Please try again in a few minutes."`.

Instead, the cashier payment flow fails to enforce the `Busy` stall check. Even when a stall on an awaiting-payment tray already has 15 open tickets (`Busy`), taking cash payment succeeds, marks the tray `Paid`, and creates a new kitchen ticket beyond the stall's capacity threshold.

---

### Steps to Reproduce \*
1. Sign in to Dapur as staff / cashier.
2. Ensure Stall 5 (`Chicken Rice Ah Lan`) has 15 open tickets (`Busy` status, `>= 15` open tickets).
3. Navigate to `/sut/mejamarmar/dapur/payments/1064` for awaiting-payment Tray `T-1064` containing an item from Stall 5 (`Chicken Rice Ah Lan`).
4. Enter a valid cash tendered amount and click `"Take payment"`.
5. Inspect the tray payment status and check Kitchen 5's active ticket queue.

---

### Expected Result \*
Per `MEJ-STL-05`, `MEJ-CTR-06`, and `MEJ-PAY-04`, `Take payment` re-checks the `Busy` status of all stalls on Tray `T-1064`, refuses to process the payment, leaves the tray unpaid, and displays `"Chicken Rice Ah Lan is busy with 15 orders. Please try again in a few minutes."`.

---

### Actual Result \*
`Take payment` succeeds without blocking, transitions Tray `T-1064` to `Paid`, and creates Ticket `091` in Kitchen 5, pushing Stall 5 past its 15-ticket `Busy` capacity limit.
