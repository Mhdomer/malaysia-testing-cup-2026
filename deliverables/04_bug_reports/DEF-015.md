# DEF-015: Stalls Page Overflows 640px Effective Viewport (200% Zoom at 1280px), Clipping Stalls 5 and 10 (`MEJ-SEC-07`)

## WBT Form Fields

- **Summary \*:** Stalls page overflows 640px effective viewport at 200% zoom (1280px window), clipping Stalls 5 and 10 (MEJ-SEC-07)
- **Area of Impact \*:** Diner Stalls / Responsive Zoom Accessibility (MEJ-SEC-07)
- **Issue Type \*:** UI
- **Severity \*:** Major
- **Priority \*:** Medium
- **Frequency \*:** Always
- **Product Risk \*:** Medium
- **Device Type:** Desktop
- **OS Version:** CachyOS Linux
- **Browser Type:** Chrome

---

### Description \*
At 200% browser zoom inside a `1280 px` desktop window (`640 px` effective CSS viewport width), the `/sut/mejamarmar/stalls` page fails to reflow its layout. It preserves a 5-column grid inside a fixed `1000 px` container, pushing the rightmost column off-screen (`x = 796 px`) and clipping Stall 5 (Chicken Rice Ah Lan) and Stall 10 (Satay Bara Senja).

---

### Steps to Reproduce \*
1. Open Chrome on a `1280 px` wide browser window and navigate to `https://www.worldbesttester.com/sut/mejamarmar/stalls`.
2. Set browser zoom to `200%` (resulting in a `640 px` CSS viewport width).
3. Inspect the stall grid layout and the visibility/position of Stall 5 (Chicken Rice Ah Lan) and Stall 10 (Satay Bara Senja).

---

### Expected Result \*
Per `MEJ-SEC-07`, at 200% zoom in a `1280 px` window (`640 px` CSS width), the layout must reflow cleanly so that every stall card and control stays visible and reachable without horizontal clipping.

---

### Actual Result \*
`/sut/mejamarmar/stalls` keeps a 5-column grid inside a fixed `1000 px` container, placing Stall 5 (Chicken Rice Ah Lan) and Stall 10 (Satay Bara Senja) at `x = 796 px` off-screen beyond the `640 px` viewport.

---

### Additional Information
- **Specification Reference:** `MEJ-SEC-07` (200% zoom visibility and reachability in a 1280 px window).
- **Affected URL:** `https://www.worldbesttester.com/sut/mejamarmar/stalls`
