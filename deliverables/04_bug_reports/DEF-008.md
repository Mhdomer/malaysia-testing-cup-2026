# DEF-008: Stall Closure Refusal Undercounts Open Tickets by Excluding Ready Tickets

- **Summary \*:** Stall Closure Refusal Undercounts Open Tickets by Excluding Ready Tickets (MEJ-STL-04)
- **Device Type:** Desktop | **OS Version:** CachyOS Linux | **Browser Type:** Chrome
- **Priority \*:** Medium
- **Severity \*:** Major
- **Area of Impact \*:** Dapur / Kitchen Display & Stall Closure
- **Frequency \*:** Always
- **Product Risk \*:** Medium
- **Issue Type \*:** Functional

---

## Description \*
When attempting to close a stall via `"Close for today"`, the refusal message undercounts the number of open tickets because it excludes tickets in the `Ready` state from the open ticket calculation, violating `MEJ-STL-04`.

Per `MEJ-STL-04`, open tickets include all tickets in `New`, `Cooking`, and `Ready` states. On Stall 1 (`Kopi & Teh Pak Zainal`), when 5 open tickets are present (`2 New` + `2 Cooking` + `1 Ready`), the closure refusal message reports only `4 open tickets`, omitting the `Ready` ticket from the count.

## Steps to Reproduce \*
1. Log in to MejaMarmar Dapur and open Stall 1 (`Kopi & Teh Pak Zainal`) Kitchen Display at `/sut/mejamarmar/dapur/kitchen/1`.
2. Ensure the stall has 5 open tickets distributed across all three active states: `2 New`, `2 Cooking`, and `1 Ready`.
3. Click `"Close for today"`.
4. Read the refusal message displayed by the system.

## Expected Result \*
Per `MEJ-STL-04`, `"Close for today"` must be refused while the stall has any open ticket (`New`, `Cooking`, or `Ready`) with the accurate total count: `"Kopi & Teh Pak Zainal still has 5 open tickets. Serve them before closing."`.

## Actual Result \*
With 5 open tickets on Stall 1 (`2 New` + `2 Cooking` + `1 Ready`), clicking `"Close for today"` displays `"Kopi & Teh Pak Zainal still has 4 open tickets. Serve them before closing."`, excluding `Ready` tickets from the open ticket count.

## Additional Information
- **Spec Reference:** `MEJ-STL-04`
- **Observed Target:** `/sut/mejamarmar/dapur/kitchen/1` (`Kopi & Teh Pak Zainal`)
- **Risk Note:** If `Ready` tickets are excluded from the open ticket check altogether, a stall with only `Ready` tickets (and `0 New` / `0 Cooking`) may be permitted to close while paid orders are still waiting for handover.
