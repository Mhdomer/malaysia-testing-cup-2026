# DEF-002: Cashier 5-Sen Rounding Rounds 6 and 7 Sen Up Instead of Down

- **Summary \*:** Cashier 5-Sen Rounding Rounds 6 and 7 Sen Up Instead of Down (MEJ-PAY-02)
- **Device Type:** Desktop | **OS Version:** CachyOS Linux | **Browser Type:** Chrome
- **Priority \*:** High
- **Severity \*:** Critical
- **Area of Impact \*:** Cashier Billing / 5-Sen Rounding Calculation
- **Frequency \*:** Always
- **Product Risk \*:** High
- **Issue Type \*:** Functional

---

## Description \*
The cashier billing calculation violates Bank Negara Malaysia (BNM) 5-sen rounding rules as defined in `MEJ-PAY-02`. Specifically, when a bill total before rounding ends in `6` sen or `7` sen, the system rounds the payable total **up** to the nearest `10` sen instead of rounding **down** to `5` sen.

This results in systematic overcharging of customers on every transaction ending in `0.06` or `0.07` RM (e.g., `RM8.67` becomes `RM8.70` instead of `RM8.65`, and `RM120.96` becomes `RM121.00` instead of `RM120.95`).

## Steps to Reproduce \*
1. Create or open a tray whose subtotal plus applicable taxes/fees produces a pre-rounding total ending in `6` or `7` sen (for example, `RM8.67` or `RM120.96`).
2. Proceed to the checkout / cashier billing summary for the tray.
3. Inspect the `Rounding` line item and the final `Total Payable` amount.

## Expected Result \*
Per `MEJ-PAY-02`, totals ending in `6` or `7` sen must round **down** to the nearest `5` sen:
- `RM8.67` rounds down to `RM8.65` (`Rounding: -RM0.02`).
- `RM120.96` rounds down to `RM120.95` (`Rounding: -RM0.01`).

## Actual Result \*
Totals ending in `6` or `7` sen round **up** to the next `10` sen:
- `RM8.67` rounds up to `RM8.70` (`Rounding: RM0.03`).
- `RM120.96` rounds up to `RM121.00` (`Rounding: RM0.04`).

## Additional Information
- **Spec Reference:** `MEJ-PAY-02`
- **Business Impact:** Direct financial discrepancy and customer overcharging; non-compliant with Malaysian 5-sen rounding standard (where 1, 2, 6, 7 sen round down and 3, 4, 8, 9 sen round up).
