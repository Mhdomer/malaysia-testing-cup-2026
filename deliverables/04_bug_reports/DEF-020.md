# DEF-020: "Send tray" Bypasses Busy Stall Check When Paying from Diner Balance (MEJ-STL-05)

## WBT Form Fields

- **Summary \*:** "Send tray" bypasses Busy stall check when paying from diner balance (MEJ-STL-05)
- **Area of Impact \*:** Diner Send Tray & Stall Capacity (MEJ-STL-05)
- **Issue Type \*:** Functional
- **Severity \*:** Critical
- **Priority \*:** High
- **Frequency \*:** Always
- **Product Risk \*:** High
- **Device Type:** Desktop
- **OS Version:** CachyOS Linux
- **Browser Type:** Chrome

---

### Description \*
Per `MEJ-STL-05`, a tray holding dishes from a `Busy` stall (`>= 15` open tickets) cannot be sent regardless of payment method, and the system must block submission with `"<stall> is busy with <n> orders. Please try again in a few minutes."`.

While `"Pay cash at the cashier"` enforces this `Busy` stall check and blocks the tray, choosing `"Pay from my balance"` completely bypasses the `Busy` check, deducts the tray total from the diner's balance, and creates a new kitchen ticket for the overloaded stall.

---

### Steps to Reproduce \*
1. Sign in as a diner with sufficient Meja Marmar balance.
2. Confirm Stall 5 (`Chicken Rice Ah Lan`) is in `Busy` state (16 open tickets).
3. Add a dish from Stall 5 to the tray and open the tray checkout / Send tray flow.
4. Select `"Pay cash at the cashier"` and attempt to send the tray. Observe that submission is blocked with the `Busy` stall error message.
5. Switch the payment method to `"Pay from my balance"` and click `"Send tray"`.
6. Observe the diner balance, tray status, and Kitchen 5 ticket queue.

---

### Expected Result \*
Per `MEJ-STL-05`, `"Send tray"` enforces the `Busy` stall check uniformly across all payment methods. Selecting `"Pay from my balance"` must block the tray, preserve the diner's balance, and display `"Chicken Rice Ah Lan is busy with 16 orders. Please try again in a few minutes."`.

---

### Actual Result \*
`"Pay cash at the cashier"` properly blocks the tray, whereas `"Pay from my balance"` bypasses the `Busy` check completely, deducts the amount from the diner's balance, marks the tray `Paid`, and creates a new ticket in Stall 5's kitchen queue.

---

### Additional Information
- **Specification Reference:** MEJ-FRS-001 v1.0 (`MEJ-STL-05`).
- **Affected Flow:** Diner Tray Checkout (`Send tray` with `"Pay from my balance"`).
