# DEF-024: Concurrent Top-Up Race Condition Bypasses RM500.00 Balance Cap (`MEJ-BAL-02`)

## WBT Form Fields

- **Summary \*:** Concurrent top-up requests bypass RM500.00 wallet cap, pushing diner balance to RM510.00 (MEJ-BAL-02)
- **Area of Impact \*:** Dapur Top-Ups & Diner Balance Cap Concurrency (MEJ-BAL-02)
- **Issue Type \*:** Functional / Concurrency
- **Severity \*:** Critical
- **Priority \*:** High
- **Frequency \*:** Always (under concurrent requests)
- **Product Risk \*:** High
- **Device Type:** Desktop
- **OS Version:** CachyOS Linux
- **Browser Type:** Chrome

---

### Description \*
Per `MEJ-BAL-02`, `"The balance after a top-up may not be above RM500.00: 'A balance can hold at most RM500.00. This diner can top up at most <amount>.', or, when not even RM10.00 fits, 'A balance can hold at most RM500.00. This diner cannot top up now.'"`.

While single sequential top-up requests exceeding `RM500.00` are rejected, the top-up endpoint `/sut/mejamarmar/dapur/topups/{diner_id}` lacks atomic locking or transactional balance serialization (TOCTOU race condition). Dispatching two parallel `RM10.00` top-up `POST` requests when a diner's balance is `RM490.00` causes both requests to pass the pre-check simultaneously and credit `+RM10.00` twice, resulting in a balance of `RM510.00` (exceeding the hard `RM500.00` top-up cap).

---

### Steps to Reproduce \*
1. Sign in to Dapur (`/sut/mejamarmar/dapur/sign-in`) as Manager or Cashier (e.g., `rosnah` / `marmar2026`).
2. Open a diner with a balance of `RM490.00` at `/sut/mejamarmar/dapur/topups/{diner_id}` (verified on Diner ID `13`, `Aina Sofea Kamal`, starting balance `RM490.00`).
3. Submit two simultaneous `POST` requests (`Promise.all`) for a top-up amount of `10` to `/sut/mejamarmar/dapur/topups/13`.
4. Reload `/sut/mejamarmar/dapur/topups/13` and check the diner's resulting balance.

---

### Expected Result \*
Per `MEJ-BAL-02` and `MEJ-SEC-06` concurrency requirements, atomic locking or serialized transaction checks must permit at most one `RM10.00` top-up (`RM490.00 -> RM500.00`) and reject the second concurrent request (`"A balance can hold at most RM500.00..."`), ensuring a topped-up balance never exceeds `RM500.00`.

---

### Actual Result \*
Both concurrent `RM10.00` top-up requests succeeded (HTTP `302`/`200`), crediting `RM20.00` total and raising the diner's balance from `RM490.00` to `RM510.00` (exceeding the `RM500.00` maximum balance cap).

---

### Additional Information
- **Specification Reference:** `MEJ-FRS-001 v1.0`, Section 9 (`MEJ-BAL-02`: `"The balance after a top-up may not be above RM500.00"`).
- **Root Cause:** Time-of-Check to Time-of-Use (TOCTOU) race condition in top-up handler without row-level DB lock or atomic balance constraint (unlike tray payment and ticket cancellation, which do enforce concurrency checks).
- **Affected URL:** `https://www.worldbesttester.com/sut/mejamarmar/dapur/topups/13`
