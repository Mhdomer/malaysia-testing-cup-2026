# DEF-017: "Sold out" Action Offered and Accepted on Final Cancelled Tickets (MEJ-TKT-02, MEJ-REF-01)

## WBT Form Fields

- **Summary \*:** "Sold out" button is active and accepted on already Cancelled tickets, transitioning final state to Refunded
- **Area of Impact \*:** Dapur / Trays & Ticket State Machine (MEJ-TKT-02, MEJ-REF-01)
- **Issue Type \*:** Functional
- **Severity \*:** Major
- **Priority \*:** Medium
- **Frequency \*:** Always
- **Product Risk \*:** Medium
- **Device Type:** Desktop
- **OS Version:** CachyOS Linux
- **Browser Type:** Chrome

---

### Description \*
Per MEJ-TKT-02 and MEJ-REF-01, `Collected`, `Cancelled`, and `Refunded` are final ticket states, and `Sold out` on a ticket line may only be offered and accepted while the ticket is `New` or `Cooking`. In another state, it must not be offered and any request must be refused (`"Ticket <number> is <state>: nothing on it can be marked sold out."`). On a Cancelled tray, the `Sold out` button remains active and transitions the `Cancelled` ticket to `Refunded`.

---

### Steps to Reproduce \*
1. Sign in to Dapur (`/sut/mejamarmar/dapur/sign-in`) as Manager (`rosnah` / `marmar2026`).
2. Open a Cancelled tray in Dapur (e.g., `/sut/mejamarmar/dapur/trays/1048`, Ticket `052`).
3. Observe the red `"Sold out"` button next to the ticket line.
4. Click `"Sold out"` and confirm the dialog.

---

### Expected Result \*
Per MEJ-TKT-02 and MEJ-REF-01, `"Sold out"` is not offered on `Cancelled` tickets, and attempting it is refused without changing the final `Cancelled` state.

---

### Actual Result \*
The `"Sold out"` button is active and clickable on Ticket `052` (state: `Cancelled`). Confirming it transitions the final `Cancelled` ticket to `Refunded` and records a `RM0.00` refund.

---
