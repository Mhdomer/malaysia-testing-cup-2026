# DEF-010: Today's "Takings in cash" Sums Unrounded Tray Totals Instead of Actual 5-Sen Rounded Amounts Paid

## WBT Form Fields

- **Summary \*:** Today's "Takings in cash" sums unrounded tray totals instead of the 5-sen rounded amounts to pay taken at the counter
- **Area of Impact \*:** Dapur / Today Financial Reporting (MEJ-DAP-07)
- **Issue Type \*:** Functional
- **Severity \*:** Critical
- **Priority \*:** High
- **Frequency \*:** Always
- **Product Risk \*:** High
- **Device Type:** Desktop
- **OS Version:** CachyOS Linux
- **Browser Type:** Chrome

---

### Description \*
Per MEJ-DAP-07, the Manager's `Today` dashboard must display "today's takings in cash (the sum of the amounts to pay taken today) and from balances (the sum of the tray totals paid from balances today)". However, the server calculates "Takings in cash" by summing the unrounded `tray_total` instead of the 5-sen rounded `amount_to_pay` actually collected at the cashier counter. Even the baseline seeded takings figure (`RM384.78`, ending in 8 sen) is mathematically impossible as a sum of 5-sen rounded cash payments.

---

### Steps to Reproduce \*
1. Sign in to Dapur (`/sut/mejamarmar/dapur/sign-in`) as Manager (`rosnah` / `marmar2026`).
2. On `/sut/mejamarmar/dapur/` (`Today`), note the initial "Takings in cash" figure (e.g. `RM384.78`).
3. Navigate to `Payments` (`/sut/mejamarmar/dapur/payments`) and open a tray awaiting cash payment whose total is affected by 5-sen rounding, such as Tray `T-1036` (Tray total: `RM7.42`, Rounding: `-RM0.02`, Amount to pay: `RM7.40`).
4. Complete "Take payment" for `T-1036` (Cash received `RM10.00`, Change `RM2.60`).
5. Return to `/sut/mejamarmar/dapur/` (`Today`) and inspect the updated "Takings in cash" value.

---

### Expected Result \*
Per MEJ-DAP-07, "Takings in cash" increases by the actual amount to pay taken at the counter (`RM7.40`), and the total is always a multiple of 5 sen.

---

### Actual Result \*
"Takings in cash" increased by `RM7.42` (the unrounded tray total) instead of `RM7.40` (the amount to pay), changing from `RM384.78` to `RM392.20`. This causes daily cash reconciliation on Dapur's Today dashboard to diverge from actual physical cash in the till on every rounded transaction.
