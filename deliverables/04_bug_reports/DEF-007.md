# DEF-007: Kitchen Display Single-Key Shortcuts S, C, H Advance Multiple Tickets Simultaneously

- **Summary \*:** Kitchen Display Single-Key Shortcuts S, C, H Advance Multiple Tickets Simultaneously (MEJ-SEC-05)
- **Device Type:** Desktop | **OS Version:** CachyOS Linux | **Browser Type:** Chrome
- **Priority \*:** High
- **Severity \*:** Major
- **Area of Impact \*:** Dapur / Kitchen Display Keyboard Shortcuts
- **Frequency \*:** Always
- **Product Risk \*:** Medium
- **Issue Type \*:** Functional

---

## Description \*
The Kitchen Display keyboard shortcut handler on `/sut/mejamarmar/dapur/kitchen/1` advances multiple tickets at once instead of advancing only the single oldest ticket in the target column, violating `MEJ-SEC-05`.

When `Shortcuts: on` is active, a single keypress of `S` (Start), `C` (Call / Ready), or `H` (Hand over) fires across multiple tickets in the corresponding column simultaneously rather than acting on the oldest ticket at most once per page load.

## Steps to Reproduce \*
1. Log in to MejaMarmar Dapur and open Stall 1 Kitchen Display at `/sut/mejamarmar/dapur/kitchen/1`.
2. Ensure multiple tickets exist in the `New`, `Cooking`, and `Ready` columns (for example, `New` tickets `212` and `214`).
3. Verify `Shortcuts: on` is enabled on the Kitchen Display.
4. Press the `S` key once and observe the `New` and `Cooking` columns.
5. Press the `C` key once and observe the `Cooking` and `Ready` columns.
6. Press the `H` key once and observe the `Ready` column.

## Expected Result \*
Per `MEJ-SEC-05`, single-key shortcuts `S` (start oldest `New` ticket), `C` (call oldest `Cooking` ticket), and `H` (hand over oldest `Ready` ticket) must act only on the single oldest ticket of each respective column, at most once per page load.

## Actual Result \*
With `Shortcuts: on` on `/sut/mejamarmar/dapur/kitchen/1`:
- Pressing `S` once transitioned multiple `New` tickets (`212` and `214`) to `Cooking` simultaneously.
- Pressing `C` once moved 2 `Cooking` tickets to `Ready` simultaneously.
- Pressing `H` once handed over 2 `Ready` tickets simultaneously.

## Additional Information
- **Spec Reference:** `MEJ-SEC-05`
- **Observed Target:** `/sut/mejamarmar/dapur/kitchen/1` (`Shortcuts: on`, Tickets `212` & `214`)
- **Operational Impact:** Causes kitchen staff to accidentally advance or hand over multiple customer orders with a single keystroke, disrupting order fulfillment tracking.
