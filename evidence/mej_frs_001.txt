1. Introduction
Scope. Meja Marmar is the ordering app of Medan Selera Meja Marmar, a food court of ten stalls. Diners build one
tray from several stalls, pay once and follow each stall's queue number on the board; Dapur ("kitchen") is the staff
console. Diners sign in with an email address, staff with a login, each with a password. Nothing is ever sent to anyone:
a new password is shown on screen in Dapur, a pay code on the tray's page. The centre's own rules below are the
authority.
Language. British English. Malay names keep their own spelling and are explained in Annex D.
Money. Malaysian ringgit, written "RM2.80": "RM" before the figure, two decimals, a comma for thousands, a hyphen
before a negative ("-RM0.02"). Figures are worked out on the server in sen, and a rule that rounds rounds half-up to
the sen unless it says otherwise. Only a cash amount is rounded to 5 sen (MEJ-PAY-02).
Typed amounts. Digits, optionally with commas between groups of three digits and a point before one or two
decimals: "15", "15.2", "15.20", "1,250" and "1,250.00" are accepted. Anything else is refused with "Type an amount in
ringgit, such as 15.20 or 1,250.00." and nothing is saved: a comma not followed by exactly three digits ("15,20",
"1,5"), a point followed by three digits ("1.250"), groups of the wrong size ("1,25,000"), both marks the continental
way ("1.250,00"), more than two decimals, spaces inside the number, signs, letters and currency symbols ("RM15").
An empty amount field says "Enter an amount."
Counting words. Every count is written with its own singular or plural: "1 open ticket", "2 open tickets", "1 cup", "1
tray".
Limits and times. "From X to Y" and "X to Y" include X and Y. "At least X", "at most X" and "up to X" include X. "More
than X", "fewer than X", "above X" and "below X" exclude X. A time of day is judged to the minute on the clock (2.29
pm lasts from 2.29.00 to 2.29.59). "From" a time includes that minute, "before" a time excludes it, and a window
from one time to another includes its first and its last minute. Times read 7.00 am, 12.00 pm (noon), 12.00 am
(midnight).
Whose clock. Every date and time is Malaysian time (Asia/Kuala_Lumpur, UTC+8, no summer time). "Today" is the
date in Kuala Lumpur and changes at midnight. No rule counts working days; public holidays change nothing.
Personas. A diner, and three roles in Dapur: Cook (of one stall), Cashier and Manager, who can do every staff step.
Testing in the app's own tab. For the address bar, Reload, Back or a second tab, open Meja Marmar in its own tab
with Open in New Tab.
Three hours. A suggested route: the diner side, then Dapur as the manager, then both together (refunds, states, the
board, limits, the address bar).
One database per team (Annex E).

3. The centre (MEJ-CTR)
MEJ-CTR-01

Halls and tables. Tables are numbered 1 to 120, each in one hall: Self-collect, where diners collect
their food at the stall, or Table service, where runners bring each Ready ticket to the table. The hall
decides the service charge (MEJ-TOT-03) and how Ready food arrives (MEJ-TKT-06). My tray and
the tray page name the table with its hall and place note: "Table <number> · <hall>" or "Table
<number> · <hall>, <note>".

MEJ-CTR-02

Out of service. A table is In use or Out of service. An out-of-service table cannot be chosen:
"Table <number> is out of service. Please choose another table."

MEJ-CTR-03

Choosing a table. On My tray the diner types a number in "Table" and presses Use this table: a
whole number from 1 to 120 ("Please enter a table number from 1 to 120."), of a table that exists
("There is no table <number>. Please choose another table.") and is In use (MEJ-CTR-02). The
figures then follow that table's hall.

MEJ-CTR-04

Open trays on a table. A table holds at most 6 open trays, an open tray being one Awaiting
payment, or Paid and not yet Complete (MEJ-TKT-04). Sending to a table that already has 6 is
refused: "Table <number> already has 6 open trays. Please choose another table." Draft,
Complete and Cancelled trays never count.

MEJ-CTR-05

Hours and last orders. The centre opens and closes at times set in Dapur, and each stall at its own
times inside them (MEJ-DAP-02). A stall takes orders from its opening time and before its last
orders, 30 minutes before its closing time. Before its opening time a tray holding its dishes cannot
be sent: "<stall> opens at <time>."; from its last orders until midnight, its closing time included:
"Last orders at <stall> were at <time>." Its tile, page and board column say one of: "Opens at
<time>" (before opening), "Open now · last orders <time>" (from opening and before last orders),
"Last orders were at <time>" (from last orders and before closing), "Closed · still serving <n>" (from
closing, while it has open tickets), "Closed · opens at <time> tomorrow" (from closing, with no
open ticket), and "Closed today" (MEJ-STL-04), which outranks the others; "Busy" (MEJ-STL-05)
replaces "Open now" while it holds.

MEJ-CTR-06

When the rules are judged. Stall hours, last orders, Closed today, Busy and teh tarik hours are
judged when the tray is sent, to the minute. A cash tray keeps the prices it was sent with; at Take
payment its stalls' hours, last orders, Closed today and Busy are checked again (MEJ-PAY-04), its
teh tarik hour prices are not.

4. Stalls and menus (MEJ-STL)
MEJ-STL-01

Stalls and their halal label. A stall has a number, a name, a cuisine, a description of up to 200
characters, a hand-painted signboard (a pair of colours), opening and closing times, a bundled
photograph and one halal label, set by the manager: "Halal certified" or "No halal certificate",
shown in words on the stall's tile, its page and its section of a tray. The label is words only (no logo,
seal, coloured badge or authority), belongs to the stall, never a dish, and is Meja Marmar's only
religious or dietary claim.

MEJ-STL-02

Dishes. A dish has a name of 2 to 60 characters, a stall, a price from RM0.50 to RM99.90 in steps of
10 sen, a description of up to 120 characters, a bundled photograph, a Teh tarik hour mark
(MEJ-TEH-02) and is Active or Inactive. An inactive dish is not listed and cannot be added: "<dish>
is no longer available." A tray line of an inactive dish shows "No longer available", and the tray
cannot be sent until it is removed: "<dish> is no longer available. Please remove it from your tray."

MEJ-STL-03

Sold out today. On the kitchen display the stall's cook or the manager presses Mark sold out on a
dish, and Mark available to undo it. A sold-out dish says "Sold out" in words on the stall's page and
cannot be added: "<dish> is sold out today."; a tray line of it shows "Sold out today", and the tray
cannot be sent until it is removed: "<dish> is sold out today. Please remove it from your tray."
Marking a ticket line sold out (MEJ-REF-01) also marks its dish sold out. Midnight does not lift the
mark.

MEJ-STL-04

Close for today. The stall's cook or the manager may Close for today. It is refused while the stall
has any open ticket (New, Cooking or Ready): "<stall> still has <open_tickets>. Serve them before
closing." (for example "2 open tickets"). A stall closed today says "Closed today"; its dishes cannot
be added ("<stall> is closed today."), and a tray holding them cannot be sent: "<stall> is closed
today. Please remove its dishes." Reopen for today undoes it. At midnight a closed stall is open
again from its opening time.

MEJ-STL-05

Busy. A stall with 15 or more open tickets (New, Cooking or Ready) is Busy: its tile, page and board
column say "Busy". Its dishes can still be put on a tray, but a tray holding them can be neither sent
nor paid at the cashier while it is Busy: "<stall> is busy with <n> orders. Please try again in a few
minutes."

MEJ-STL-06

Listing. Stalls are listed in number order; each tile shows the signboard, the cuisine, the halal label
and the state in words (MEJ-CTR-05). A stall that is inactive in Dapur is not listed, and its dishes
cannot be added: "<stall> is not taking orders." The diner side offers no search and no filters.

5. The tray (MEJ-TRY)
MEJ-TRY-01

One tray, lines and quantities. A diner builds one tray at a time, a Draft. Dishes are added on a
stall's page with a "Quantity" from 1 to 9 ("Choose a quantity from 1 to 9."), Dine in (chosen at first) or
Tapau, and Add to tray: "<dish> is on your tray." Adding a dish already on the tray with the same
choice adds to its line. A quantity above 9, or a line passing 9 of its dish, gets "You can have at most
9 of one dish on a line." A signed-out visitor sees the prices and Sign in to order.

MEJ-TRY-02

Five stalls. A tray holds dishes from at most 5 stalls. Adding a dish from a further stall is refused: "A
tray can hold dishes from up to 5 stalls. Send this tray first, then start another."

MEJ-TRY-03

Dine in and Tapau. Every line is Dine in or Tapau. On My tray a line moves between the marble table
and the tapau bag by dragging its dish, by the keyboard (Space picks it up, Space on the bag or
table drops it) or with the line's Tapau or Dine in button. Moving a line onto a line of the same dish
with the other choice merges the two, keeping a note either carries. A move that would pass
MEJ-TRY-01's limit of 9 is refused with that message, one joining two different notes with "The two
lines of <dish> have different notes. Change or clear one of them first.", and nothing moves.

MEJ-TRY-04

Notes. A line may carry a note for the stall of up to 60 characters ("A note has at most 60
characters."), typed in "Note for the stall" or picked from the suggestions "Less sweet", "Less
spicy", "No vegetables" and "Extra ice", and saved with Save note. Any letters and symbols may be
typed. It reaches the kitchen display, both tray pages and the receipt exactly as typed.

MEJ-TRY-05

My tray. My tray groups the lines by stall, in stall-number order, each a plate on the marble table,
with each section's figures (MEJ-TOT) and the tray total. A Draft shows today's prices, and the teh
tarik hour price during a teh tarik hour. A line's quantity changes with "Quantity" and Update (1 to 9);
Remove takes a line off and Empty tray takes every line off, each after confirmation. An empty
Draft says "Your tray is empty."

MEJ-TRY-06

Send tray. On Send tray the diner sees the tray, table and figures, chooses Pay from my balance
or Pay cash at the cashier ("Please choose how you pay: from your balance or in cash at the
cashier.") and presses Send tray. The checks run in this order, the first failure shown with nothing
changed: the tray has a line ("Your tray is empty.") and a table ("Please choose a table on My tray
first."); the table (MEJ-CTR-02, -03, -04); each stall's hours and last orders (MEJ-CTR-05), in stall
order; Closed today (MEJ-STL-04); Busy (MEJ-STL-05); inactive or sold-out lines (MEJ-STL-02,
-03); then a balance payment (MEJ-PAY-01).

MEJ-TRY-07

What sending fixes. Sending fixes the unit prices, which cups get the teh tarik hour price
(MEJ-TEH-03), the table, its hall and service, and every figure; nothing changed later in Dapur alters
a sent tray. A tray takes its number, "T-" and four digits, when first sent, the number after the
highest so far, and keeps it if sent again (MEJ-PAY-05).

6. Teh tarik hours (MEJ-TEH)
MEJ-TEH-01

The windows. Teh tarik hours are daily windows, each from a start minute to an end minute of one
day, set in Dapur (Annex C). Home and the Kopi & Teh stall's page say "Teh tarik hour now, until
<end>", "Next teh tarik hour at <start>" or "No more teh tarik hours today".

MEJ-TEH-02

The price. During a teh tarik hour, every dish marked Teh tarik hour costs RM1.50 instead of its
menu price, dine in or tapau. Every other dish keeps its menu price.

MEJ-TEH-03

Two cups a tray. At most 2 cups on a tray get the teh tarik hour price. When the tray holds more
marked cups, the 2 dearest by menu price get it; between cups of the same menu price, those on
the line added to the tray first.

MEJ-TEH-04

Setting the windows. The manager adds, edits and deletes windows: whole minutes, the end after
the start on the same day, each from 30 minutes to 3 hours long counting its first and last minute
("A teh tarik hour lasts from 30 minutes to 3 hours."), no window overlapping another ("Teh tarik
hours cannot overlap."), and at most 4 windows ("There can be at most 4 teh tarik hours.").

7. Totals (MEJ-TOT)
Each stall's section of a tray (later its ticket) is priced on its own and shows "Items", "Containers", "Service charge",
"Service tax" and "Section total", and the tray its "Tray total". The diner's pages leave out Containers or Service
charge of RM0.00, but show Service charge at a Table service table.
MEJ-TOT-01

Items. Items is the sum over the section's lines of unit price times quantity, using the teh tarik hour
price for the cups MEJ-TEH-02 and -03 give it.

MEJ-TOT-02

Containers. Containers is RM0.30 for every dish on a Tapau line; Dine-in lines use none.

MEJ-TOT-03

Service charge. Service charge is 10 % of the section's Dine-in Items, only when the tray's table
stands in a hall with Table service; Tapau lines and containers never carry it. Rounded half-up to the
sen.

MEJ-TOT-04

Service tax. Service tax is 6 % of (Items + Containers + Service charge), rounded half-up to the
sen.

MEJ-TOT-05

Section and tray totals. A section total is Items + Containers + Service charge + Service tax; the
tray total is the sum of the section totals. The browser only shows the figures.

MEJ-TOT-06

The same figures everywhere. The same figures appear on My tray, Send tray, the tray page, the
receipt, Dapur's Take payment and Dapur's tray page.

8. Paying (MEJ-PAY)
MEJ-PAY-01

Pay from my balance. Pay from my balance takes the tray total from the diner's balance, to the
sen, at once, and the tray is Paid: "Tray <number> is paid. Watch your numbers on the board." It is
refused when the balance is less than the tray total: "Your balance is <balance> and this tray is
<total>. Top up at the cashier or pay cash." (for example "Your balance is RM5.60 and this tray is
RM6.89. Top up at the cashier or pay cash."). A balance never goes below RM0.00.

MEJ-PAY-02

Cash and the 5-sen rounding. Pay cash at the cashier asks for the amount to pay: the tray total
rounded to the nearest 5 sen. By the tray total's last digit of sen:

MEJ-PAY-03

Awaiting payment. A cash tray becomes Awaiting payment with a four-digit pay code, unique
among the trays awaiting payment, shown large on its page: "Pay <amount> at the cashier with
code <code>." Its tickets are made only when the payment is taken.

MEJ-PAY-04

Take payment. A cashier or the manager finds the tray on Payments, in its list or by "Pay code" and
Find ("Enter the 4-digit pay code.", "No tray is awaiting payment with code <code>."). Take
payment shows the lines, tray total, rounding and amount to pay of its latest sending. The cashier
types "Cash received", at least the amount to pay and in steps of 5 sen ("Cash received must be at
least <amount> and in steps of 5 sen."), and presses Take payment. The hours, last orders, Closed
today and Busy of its stalls are checked again (MEJ-CTR-06); then the tray is Paid, its tickets are
made, and the page says "Payment taken. Change <amount>."

MEJ-PAY-05

Edit tray and Cancel unpaid tray. While a tray is Awaiting payment, the diner may press Edit tray,
after confirmation: it goes back to Draft ("Tray <number> is back on My tray. Send it again when it is
ready."), its pay code stops working, and sending it again gives a new amount to pay and a new
code. Edit tray is refused while the diner is building another tray: "Send or empty the tray you are
building first." A cashier or the manager may press Cancel unpaid tray, after confirmation: the tray
becomes Cancelled, which is final ("Tray <number> has been cancelled."). Neither is offered, nor
accepted, once the tray is Paid: "Tray <number> is <state>: it can no longer be changed."

MEJ-PAY-06

Two ways to pay. The balance and cash at the cashier are the only ways to pay.

9. Balance and top-ups (MEJ-BAL)
MEJ-BAL-01

The balance. Every diner has a Meja Marmar balance, starting at RM0.00: the sum of its
movements (top-ups, payments and refunds). Balance shows it with the history, newest first: date,
time, what, amount and the balance after.

MEJ-BAL-02

Top-ups. The diner pays the cashier in cash, and a cashier or the manager records the top-up in
Dapur with "Amount" and Top up, in steps of RM10.00, from RM10.00 ("Top-ups are made in steps
of RM10.00."): "<name> has been topped up by <amount>. The balance is now <balance>." The
balance after a top-up may not be above RM500.00: "A balance can hold at most RM500.00. This
diner can top up at most <amount>.", or, when not even RM10.00 fits, "A balance can hold at most
RM500.00. This diner cannot top up now."

MEJ-BAL-03

Refunds. A refund is always credited to the balance, in full, whatever the tray was paid with and
whatever the balance already holds.

MEJ-BAL-04

Nothing else moves a balance. Only top-ups, payments and refunds move a balance; it cannot be
withdrawn, transferred or edited.

Tickets and queue numbers. Paying a tray (from the balance at Send tray, or in cash at Take
payment) makes one ticket per stall on it, with that stall's next queue number. Each stall counts
001 to 999, and after 999 comes 001; queue numbers are always shown as three digits and do not
start again each day.

Collected, Cancelled and Refunded are final. An action is offered only in its own state; asked for in another it is
refused with the ticket and its state: "Ticket <number> is <state>: it cannot be started.", "Ticket <number> is
<state>: it cannot be called." or "Ticket <number> is <state>: it cannot be handed over."
MEJ-TKT-03

One ticket at a time. Every action acts on one ticket only: the other tickets of the same tray keep
their states, and one ticket's money never moves another's.

MEJ-TKT-04

Complete. A tray is Complete when every one of its tickets is Collected, Cancelled or Refunded;
its page then says "Everything has been collected", or, when something was refunded, "Complete.
Refunded: <refunds>." with each refund as "<stall> <amount>", and it stops counting on its table
(MEJ-CTR-04). Until then a paid tray reads "Paid".

MEJ-TKT-05

The kitchen display. A stall's kitchen display shows its open tickets only, in three columns, New,
Cooking and Ready, each oldest first (by time paid, then ticket): queue number, table, and each
line with its quantity, dish, "Tapau" where it is and its note. A Cancelled or Refunded ticket leaves at
once. The display also holds the stall's dishes with Mark sold out or Mark available, its message on
the board (MEJ-BRD-05), the single-key shortcuts (MEJ-SEC-05), Close for today or Reopen for
today, and Watch on the board.

MEJ-TKT-06

The diner's tray page. The diner's tray page shows each ticket's stall, queue number in split-flap
digits and state in words: "Waiting for the stall" (New), "Cooking", "Ready: collect at stall
<number>" (a Self-collect hall) or "Ready: on its way to table <number>" (a Table service hall),
"Collected", "Cancelled, refunded <amount>" and "Sold out, refunded <amount>" (for example
"Cancelled, refunded RM7.15").

11. The board (MEJ-BRD)
MEJ-BRD-01

What the board shows. The board is public. It shows every stall's number, name, signboard and
state in words (MEJ-CTR-05); for a stall open now, or past closing with open tickets, two rows:
"Cooking" (its New and Cooking numbers) and "Ready" (its Ready numbers), every number, oldest
first. A stall past its closing time shows "Closed · still serving <n>" with its numbers until they are
handed over; a stall closed today shows "Closed today" and no numbers. The board shows no
names, tables or amounts.

MEJ-BRD-02

Split-flap numbers, re-read. Numbers are split-flap digits. An open board re-reads its numbers
every 20 seconds without reloading the page, and on Reload; re-reading changes nothing. Digits
that changed since this browser's last view flip; on a browser's first view every flap settles once; a
Kopi & Teh number that has just become Ready is greeted by a teh tarik poured from a raised jug.
Each movement plays once, under five seconds. The board changes only when a ticket changes or
a stall's hours pass.

MEJ-BRD-03

Pause and reduced motion. Pause updates stops the re-reading and every movement until
Resume updates is pressed; with the browser set to reduce motion, nothing flips, settles or pours,
and the numbers change at once.

MEJ-BRD-04

Yours. A signed-in diner's own numbers are marked "Yours", in words and with an outline.

MEJ-BRD-05

Message on the board. The stall's cook or the manager may set a message on the board of up to
60 characters ("A message has at most 60 characters.") from the kitchen display with "Message on
the board" and Save message; it shows under the stall's name on the board and on its page; saving
it empty clears it. Any letters and symbols may be typed.

12. Sold out, cancellation and refunds (MEJ-REF)
MEJ-REF-01

Sold out on a line. The stall's cook or the manager presses Sold out on a ticket line on the kitchen
display, and the manager also on Dapur's tray page, only while its ticket is New or Cooking; in
another state it is not offered, and a request is refused: "Ticket <number> is <state>: nothing on it
can be marked sold out."

MEJ-REF-02

The sold-out refund. A sold-out line is refunded in full, service charge and tax included: the
section total before, less the section total again without the line, every other line at its sent price. It
is credited to the balance (MEJ-BAL-03), and the line shows "Sold out, refunded <amount>" (for
example "Sold out, refunded RM8.35"). When every line of a ticket is sold out, the ticket is
Refunded.

MEJ-REF-03

Cancel this ticket. The diner may press Cancel this ticket while it is New, after confirmation; the
refund is that ticket's section total. Once the stall has started it: "<stall> has started ticket
<number>. It can no longer be cancelled."; once it is final: "Ticket <number> is <state>: it cannot be
cancelled."

MEJ-REF-04

Exact figures. Refunds are worked out on the exact figures: the 5-sen rounding of a cash payment
is never refunded or charged back.

MEJ-REF-05

Where refunds show. Every refund appears on the diner's Balance history, on the tray page and on
Dapur's tray page, with its time and amount.

13. Accounts (MEJ-ACC)
MEJ-ACC-01

Sign in. Diners sign in on Meja Marmar's Sign in with "Email" (whatever its capitals) and "Password";
staff on Dapur's with "Login" and "Password". A refused sign-in says "The email or password is not

MEJ-SEC-03

The server decides. Figures come from stored data; nothing the browser sends as a price,
amount, table, state or queue number is trusted beyond what the person chose or typed, and an
action whose control is not offered is refused when posted.

MEJ-SEC-04

Text as typed. Free text (names, notes, descriptions, place notes, board messages) is stored and
shown exactly as typed, in any script, symbols and emoji included, always as text, never as markup.

MEJ-SEC-05

Keyboard, labels, colour and pointer. Every control has a visible label, works by keyboard and
shows its focus. Nothing is told by colour alone: Busy, Sold out, Closed today and every state are
words. Everything done by dragging can also be done with a single click (the line's Tapau and Dine
in buttons). The kitchen display's single-key shortcuts, S (start the oldest New ticket), C (call the
oldest Cooking ticket) and H (hand over the oldest Ready ticket), act only while the focus is in no
text field or list, each at most once per page load; the display says "Shortcuts: on" or "Shortcuts:
off", and Turn shortcuts off and Turn shortcuts on switch them for that member of staff.

MEJ-SEC-06

Safe returns, forms and confirmations. Sign-in returns only to pages of Meja Marmar itself
(MEJ-ACC-01). Every form that changes data redirects after its post, so Reload and Back never
repeat it. A form refused for a field shows again with every value typed (never a password), the
message beside that field and the focus on it; a refusal about the record itself (it changed, a stall
became busy or closed) shows at the top of the page, redrawn from the record's current state.
Two people taking the same tray number, pay code, email, login or table at the same moment
never both succeed: the second is told "Someone else did this at the same moment. Please try
again." and nothing is saved. Every irreversible action (Remove, Empty tray, Edit tray, Cancel this
ticket, Cancel unpaid tray, Sold out, Mark sold out, Close for today, Deactivate, Delete, Reset
password, Reset data) asks for confirmation once, in one dialog that names what will happen.

MEJ-SEC-07

Laptop first. Every page works from 1024 to 1440 px wide without horizontal scrolling, and at 200
% zoom in a 1280 px window every control stays visible and reachable; the diner pages stay usable
at 375 px.
